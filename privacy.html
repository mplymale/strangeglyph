Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy covers strangeglyph.com, the website of Strange Glyph, LLC (“Strange Glyph,” “we,” “us”). It's short because this site collects very little.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. This site has no accounts, no forms, no analytics, no advertising, and sets no cookies.

Services this site relies on

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operational purposes, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts. Typefaces load from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers. See Google's privacy policy.

If you email us

If you write to info@strangeglyph.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply and to handle your request. We don't sell it or add you to a mailing list. Ask us to delete your messages and we will.

Our apps

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.
