About the studio

Small by
design.

No meetings. Just work. Strange Glyph makes apps. Not decks. Not roadmaps. Not alignment meetings. Apps.

We're an independent studio, which means the person who had the idea is the same person who wrote the code, obsessed over the icon, and quietly changed the button radius at 2am. On purpose.

Everything ships when it's ready. Which takes as long as it takes.
